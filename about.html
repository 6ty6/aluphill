About Aluphill

Aluphill Cladding Company Ltd is a Ghanaian-owned company established in 2014 and registered in 2018. We specialize in aluminium, cladding, and finishing works.

Our strength lies in precision, quality workmanship, and a commitment to delivering durable and modern construction solutions.
